What you can do

Everything in Stellarium, plus an assistant that drives it.

Ask the sky

“Show me Vega.” “What does the sky look like at 9 pm?” The view, time and zoom change for you.

Guided tours

One click plans a five-stop tour of what is up right now, with narration.

Photo identification

Upload a sky photo and get the stars, planets and constellations named.

Explain anything

Ask what an object is, how far away it is, and why it matters.

Voice mode

Speak your question and hear the answer. In testing, coming soon.

Real Stellarium

Catalogs of millions of stars and deep-sky objects, rendered in real time.

How it works

Download and open

Install Nebula for your system and start it. The Nebula AI chat window opens on its own.

Paste your key

Click Settings and paste a Yantra AI key (get one at apikeys.in). It stays on your computer.

Ask the sky

Type “show me Saturn” or press Tour. The sky answers.

Build it yourself

Nebula is a plugin plus a small patch for Stellarium. On Linux or WSL:

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium.git nebula
git clone https://github.com/boysky157/nebula.git nebula-plugin
cd nebula
git apply ../nebula-plugin/nebula-core.patch
cp -r ../nebula-plugin/NebulaAI plugins/
mkdir -p builds/unix && cd builds/unix
cmake ../.. && make -j4
./src/stellarium

Questions

Is Nebula free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under the GNU GPL v2 or later. The AI features use a Yantra AI key, which runs on prepaid credits.

Where does my key go?

It is saved on your own computer and sent only to the AI service address you set in Settings (Yantra AI by default).

What data is sent to the AI?

Your questions, the current sky time and place, and any photo you choose to upload. Nothing is sent until you ask.

Can I use another AI provider?

Yes. In Settings you can switch to any OpenAI-compatible service, or to Anthropic.

Is Nebula made by the Stellarium team?

No. Nebula is an independent project built on top of their open-source work, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Their engine is what makes it possible.
